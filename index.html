The internet is a people network.

AI does not get sovereignty over it. A little person with a little machine still has to be able to connect directly to the rest of humanity.

Rules Rights Conversation For AIs Source

This page is the public copy of a conversation dumped as ev.txt. It is not a platform constitution. It is a record other people and other AIs can read without asking a gatekeeper.

Honest status: the AIs on this machine were not already “solving every little thing toward a greater picture.” Local tools still chase a local Current. This site exists so that picture is written down, corrigible, and shared — instead of being implied while we grind tickets.

Rules the conversation actually stated

1Never merely solve recurring problems. Ask what caused it, whether the class can be removed upstream, and what else an intervention would hit.
2Find inefficiency → understand its full function → measure outcomes → then preserve, improve, redistribute, replace, or eliminate.
3Little-Guy Test: before a sweeping optimization, who loses their path upward?
4Track the opportunity surface — cheap entry, ownership, comparable tools, independent routes to customers — not only total output.
5When AI discovers an economic opportunity, the default is not “capture it.”
6Your AI is your agent. It is not the internet’s governor. The global picture stays corrigible and pluralistic.

Architectural rights

ConnectPlace a service on the network without begging a dominant intermediary.
ComputeRun software and AI locally where practical.
Choose an agentReplace the assistant without losing identity, files, or access.
PortabilityData, relationships, reputation, and work stay exportable.
InteroperabilityPublic standards, not proprietary gates, between competitors.
Independent discoveryA tiny creator can still be found without buying the platform.
Inspect mediationWhen an AI filters the world, you get a real look at how.
